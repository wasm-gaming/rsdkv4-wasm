## The common part (all the engines with a settings screen)

- **engine-specs `^0.3.2` → `^0.6.0`**. The app imports the helpers from `../engine-specs.js`, the
  runtime copy the Makefile already ships beside the SDK.
- **English and Spanish**:
  - every string of the app (today Spanish, inline) moves into one `{ en, es }` table;
  - the table is read through `host.locale ?? resolveLocale()`;
  - SDK errors stay English and are shown as a detail under the app's own words.
- **Pause menu**: Continuar, the engine's entries, **Ajustes**, **Pantalla completa**, Volver al
  lanzador (UX.md rule 4).
- **Ajustes**: `createSettingsScreen()`.
  - Rows come from `settingsModel()` over the SDK's `config()` / `options()` descriptors (only those
    with a `label`), plus `SHARED_SETTINGS`.
  - A live change is applied at once.
  - A non-live one is saved and passed at the next start; its row says "Se aplica al reiniciar".
  - Choices are saved per engine with `saveSettings('rsdkv4')` and read at mount with
    `loadSettings()`.
- **Fullscreen**: `createFullscreenToggle(root)` (Cmd+Enter / Alt+Enter, Select + Y), plus the
  pause-menu entry.
- **Performance**:
  - `createWakeLock()`, held while playing when "Mantener la pantalla encendida" is on (default
    on);
  - `createPerfCounter()` when "Mostrar rendimiento" is on: fed once per emulated frame where the
    SDK reports frames, else counting display frames.
- **Controls and audio pacing**: per engine, below.
- **Checks**:
  - `make test` / build;
  - in Chromium, with the owner's test ROMs where needed: the pause menu, every setting applied (or
    noted as on restart), saved across a reload, fullscreen by key and by menu, the counter, en / es;
  - a phone width.
- **Release**: minor.

## RSDKv4

- **The bump crosses 0.4 and 0.5**: to check as for BlastEm.
- **Settings**: new `config.renderFilter` (CSS `image-rendering`, live).
  - It is **pixelated by default**, like v3 and v5. Today the canvas is CSS-scaled with nothing set,
    so the browser smooths it.
  - Vídeo → "Suavizado".
- **Controls**: feasible, applied on restart.
  - `src/rsdkv4.options.ts` already writes `KeyboardEvent.code` values into `settings.ini`'s
    `[Keyboard 1]`.
  - The app lists Up, Down, Left, Right, A, B, C, Start.
  - The gamepad goes through SDL's own mapping: to check whether `[Controller 1]` can take it.
- **Audio pacing**: not offered (the engine's own loop).
- **Order**: sixth.

## Walkthrough (2026-11-18 00:40 UTC) — RSDKv4 done

Adds: what was built, what was settled from the plan's "to check" items, and the checks.

**Settled**:
- **The bump crosses 0.4 and 0.5 without changes**: `tsc` is clean and the tests pass on engine-specs
  0.6.1.
- **Controls: live, not on restart.** The plan said `settings.ini` (`[Keyboard 1]`, read once at
  boot). The engine reads keys and pads through Emscripten's SDL2 on the page, so BlastEm's way
  applies instead (`src/rsdkv4.controls.ts`): remapped keys are replayed as the engine's defaults,
  and pad buttons are reordered in a wrapped `getGamepads()`. The gamepad is covered too.
- The controls are the ones the Sonics read: the d-pad, A, B, C and Start, with `RSDKV4_INPUT`'s
  defaults.

**SDK** (`src/`):
- `rsdkv4.options.ts`: `config.renderFilter` (`'pixelated'` by default, live, Vídeo "Suavizado").
  The canvas was smoothed by the browser until now.
- `rsdkv4.sdk.ts`: the filter on the canvas (at mount and on change), `play.setControls()`, the key
  listener and the `getGamepads` wrapper.
- `rsdkv4.controls.ts`: `RSDKV4_CONTROLS`, `controlTables()`, `remapPad()`.

**App** (`src/app/app.js`, `app.css`): RSDKv4's own app (slots + pause menu) gets the same parts as
the emulators:
- every string in a `STRINGS` table (en / es), the pack screens included. The slot cards' NO SAVE /
  PLAY / CONTINUE stay as the games write them;
- the pause menu: Continuar, Cambiar de partida, the pack's screens, **Ajustes**, **Pantalla
  completa**, Volver al lanzador;
- Ajustes (`createSettingsScreen`): Suavizado, the shared rows and Controles. The pack's GAME
  OPTIONS (no labels) stay in the game's own menu;
- saved in OPFS `settings/rsdkv4.json`; the config goes in at boot and the controls after start;
- fullscreen, the Wake Lock while in game, and the counter (display frames).

**Checks**:

  smoothing descriptor, remapping with swaps). That test found the `remapPad` fix, applied to
